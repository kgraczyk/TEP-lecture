01 / ABOUT THE COURSE

Understand the why
behind the equations.

This graduate-level course develops the foundations of theoretical particle physics through explicit derivations, symmetry principles and physical applications. Rather than presenting the Standard Model as a set of formulas, the lectures build its structure step by step.

The materials cover scattering theory, group representations, the quark model, QCD, gauge symmetry, electroweak unification, flavor physics and neutrino masses.

The slides accompany classroom lectures: some intermediate reasoning and interpretations may be explained orally.

03 / PREPARATION

Prerequisites

This course is intended for graduate physics students. Familiarity with quantum mechanics, special relativity and basic quantum field theory is recommended.
