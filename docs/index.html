Graduate lecture course

Theory of Elementary Particles

Krzysztof M. Graczyk

An introduction to theoretical particle physics, from scattering amplitudes and symmetry principles to gauge theories, the Standard Model, and neutrino physics.

The course emphasizes mathematical derivations and the theoretical reasoning behind particle physics. The lecture slides have been developed and updated over successive editions of the course at the University of Wrocław.
